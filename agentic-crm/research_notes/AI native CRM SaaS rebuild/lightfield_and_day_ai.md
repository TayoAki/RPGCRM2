# Lightfield and Day AI: two AI-native CRMs (product, pricing, architecture, company), as of 2026-11-10

Scope note: Lightfield moved its marketing site from lightfield.app to lightfield.com (301 redirects); the app and docs still live at crm.lightfield.app and docs.lightfield.app. Day AI is at day.ai. Most product facts below come from the vendors' own docs, pricing pages, job posts, MCP/SDK repos and trust pages, read on 2026-11-10 unless dated otherwise. Each vendor's comparison page about the other is self-interested and flagged as such.

## 1. Positioning: what "AI-native CRM" means to each, who it is for, how it differs from Salesforce/HubSpot/Attio

### Takeaway
Lightfield sells itself as a full replacement CRM ("CRM 3.0", a "business world model") that builds its own records from email, calendar, meetings and messaging. It now targets funded startups up to Series C and is sold sales-led with hands-on deployment. Day AI has moved from "AI-native CRM" (2024) to "CRMx" (Feb 2026) to "context graph and agent control plane" / "Life After CRM" (late 2026). It runs either as the CRM or as a layer of agents and memory on top of Salesforce or HubSpot, priced per agent.

### Cited Findings
**Lightfield**
- Calls itself an "AI-native CRM" whose goal is a "business world model" built from customer interactions. It starts with CRM and plans to expand toward budgets, capacity and market conditions. — [Lightfield homepage](https://lightfield.com)
- Job posts describe the company as "reimagining CRM as a world model of a business. By learning from emails, meetings, and customer conversations, we're building a living understanding of how a company works—so AI can help teams anticipate what comes next, make better decisions, and take action." — [Lightfield Ashby job board](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Lightfield)
- Keith Peiris (CEO): "Companies that love us the most treat us like their business world model" and "We are becoming the oracle to the executive team." The planned expansion runs from sales to operations and finance, then engineering. — [Upstarts Media, Sep 9 2026](https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/ai-sales-startup-lightfield-raises-47m)
- On the a16z podcast, co-host Alex Rampell named "intelligence is greater than schema" as "the crux". Peiris: "If we can help you completely model your business and your customer reality, then the rest will be easy." — [a16z Show "The AI-Native CRM", Sep 16 2026 (transcript)](https://podscripts.co/podcasts/the-a16z-show/the-ai-native-crm)
- Peiris calls the data model "CRM 3.0", "designed to absorb everything about a customer", in contrast to older CRMs with fixed objects whose fields humans fill in. He describes the target customer as a Series C company. — [Open Source CEO interview, Jul 21 2026](https://www.opensourceceo.com/p/keith-peiris-interview)
- Peiris called legacy CRM "perhaps the most complex and lowest satisfaction piece of software on Earth." — [Lightfield press post of VentureBeat coverage, Nov 20 2025](https://lightfield.com/blog/venturebeat-tome-s-founders-ditch-viral-presentation-app-with-20m-users-to-build-ai-native-crm)
- Not self-serve at scale: "sales-led consultative implementation" with forward-deployed engineers and ex-consultants, and a target implementation time of 30 days. Many customers run Salesforce alongside Lightfield at first. Named rivals include Attio, Reevo, Apollo, Unify, Clay and Monaco. — [Upstarts Media](https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/ai-sales-startup-lightfield-raises-47m)
- Customer success copy: "For teams who go deep, Lightfield replaces a whole stack: the CRM, the enrichment, the sequencing, and the operator who wired it all together." — [Lightfield CSM job post](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Lightfield/81aa566c-356e-4938-a6f6-4e94eb5b6a42)
- Named testimonial customers: SF Compute, Macroscope, Power, Intent HQ, Agree, Verdigris, Kaizen and Weaver. Macroscope replaced Clay with Lightfield. — [Lightfield homepage](https://lightfield.com); [Upstarts Media](https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/ai-sales-startup-lightfield-raises-47m)
- a16z called Lightfield "the CRM for the agentic era" in its investment post. Authorship is reported inconsistently (Schmidt/Rampell/Serafini or Schmidt/Rampell). — [Unite.ai via search](https://unite.ai/lightfield-raises-47m-series-a-led-by-a16z-to-accelerate-growth/)

**Day AI**
- Current self-description: "Day AI provides the context graph and agent control plane to quickly and securely upgrade your company brain… Day AI is what comes after CRM… It runs in place of Salesforce or HubSpot or as a layer on top of them." It is for "Revenue teams at B2B companies, from founder-led startups to scale-ups with a full-time RevOps owner." — [Day AI llms.txt](https://day.ai/llms.txt)

- Series A post (Feb 2 2026): software built for AI agents as the primary user, with a "context graph" designed for agents to navigate. It argues that rival "AI-native" systems add transcription and summaries on top of incomplete data. Startups can replace their CRM, and scaling companies can run Day AI alongside one. — [Day AI Series A post](https://www.day.ai/resources/series-a-and-the-beginning-of-the-shift-in-crm)
- "This is the Cursor for CRM, it's the same pattern": delegate high-effort work a human wouldn't want to do full-time. O'Donnell: "Salesforce as we think of it today is not going to exist in two or three years." — [Upstarts Media, Feb 2 2026](https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/day-ai-sequoia-ai-crm)

- Lightfield's comparison (Feb 19 2026, biased) says Day AI is "retrieval-focused" and summarizes rather than explains "why". It calls Day AI's tiers complex and the skill-slot concept unclear. — [Lightfield blog: Day AI review](https://lightfield.com/blog/day-ai-review)

### Inferences
- The two now differ less in features than in deployment model. Lightfield is a single system of record with its own sequencer, enrichment and recorder, sold with heavy services. Day AI is an agent and memory layer that can sit over an incumbent CRM through MCP and bill per agent, not per human.
- Both have moved from founders and small startups toward scale-ups (Series C at Lightfield; "20 to 200 people" scale-ups at Day AI, per its guide titles in [llms.txt](https://day.ai/llms.txt)).

### Gaps
- Neither vendor publishes a feature-by-feature comparison with Attio beyond competitor mentions. Attio is named only as a rival (Upstarts) and in Day AI's ranked SEO guide, which I did not read.

## 2. How data gets in, auto-built records, data model and "memory"

### Takeaway
Both ingest mailbox, calendar, a native meeting bot, Slack and third-party transcripts (Gong, Granola), and both auto-create people and companies. Lightfield deliberately does not auto-create opportunities; Day AI does, with a toggle. Lightfield stores data as a schema-flexible entity-attribute-value graph on Postgres with versioned values and an activity log. Day AI exposes a typed object graph (`native_*` types with edges and per-edge properties), versioned property history, and a source citation on every value.

### Cited Findings
**Lightfield: ingestion**
- Connects email, calendar, Slack, LinkedIn "and other systems", and captures interactions automatically. — [Lightfield homepage](https://lightfield.com)
- Settings → Integrations covers Granola, Gong, LinkedIn (DMs), Microsoft Teams, Slack and WhatsApp (1:1 DMs). These are "imports data into the CRM" connections, separate from MCP connectors (Notion, Linear, Granola, Stripe), which the agent queries on demand. — [Lightfield docs: Integrations](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/settings/integrations/)
- Mail and calendar: Google or Outlook. Every plan includes "email, Slack and Microsoft Teams sync". — [Lightfield docs: Billing](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/settings/billing/)
- Native recorder joins Zoom, Google Meet and Teams calls on a synced calendar. The default is "Record external meetings only". Inviting recorder@lightfield.app forces recording. — [Lightfield docs: Recording](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/settings/recording/)
- No built-in dialer and no automatic phone-call logging, and no native mobile app (mobile web only). — [Lightfield docs: Integrations](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/settings/integrations/)
- Record creation: one Account per company domain and one Contact per person. The per-mailbox setting is Off / When someone responds / When you reach out (default) / Always. Internal, automated (unsubscribe/bulk headers, noreply@) and personal-domain mail are filtered, and blocked domains and "exclusion categories" also apply. "Lightfield never creates opportunities on its own from email or meetings. You, the agent, an automation or the API create them." — [Lightfield docs: How records are created](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/settings/mail-and-calendar/how-records-are-created/)
- Fields update from new activity through each field's AI fill setting (Off / Suggest / Auto) and an optional AI prompt. Suggest writes nothing until someone approves it on the record. The agent can backfill past emails and meetings ("Fill in Current CRM on all open opportunities from past meetings and emails"). — [Lightfield docs: Data model](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/settings/data-model/)
- Mailbox privacy: each mailbox is Full access or Metadata only, and items can be made private. Metadata only "can't hide an item that a teammate's Full access account also synced." — quoted from Lightfield help in [Day AI vs Lightfield](https://day.ai/guides/day-ai-vs-lightfield) (Lightfield's own page was not read directly)
- Scale: "We ingest tens of millions of emails and calendar events a month." — [Lightfield Backend (Staff+) job](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Lightfield/46e9843d-3b60-4f54-b4f8-ae8784559383)

**Lightfield: data model and memory**
- "Customers define their own objects, attributes, and relationships at runtime, so the core data model is a schema-flexible, graph-shaped store: entity-attribute-value with typed edges, versioned attribute values, and relationship history." — [Lightfield Data Infrastructure (Staff+) job](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Lightfield/094fac84-e417-4fd7-b9fd-49d7efec8a2e)
- "Lightfield builds a living model of a company's customers from emails, calls, meetings, and other signals. It structures that raw history into customer-defined objects, attributes, relationships, and versioned values, then exposes the same underlying capabilities to the product UI, external APIs, automations, and agents." — [Lightfield Backend (Staff+) job](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Lightfield/46e9843d-3b60-4f54-b4f8-ae8784559383)
- Podcast account: the system started as a chronological activity log modeled on the Facebook timeline (outreach, messages, meetings, documents, product usage, payments). Fully unstructured storage made queries too slow, so it is now semi-structured: unstructured data lives in the activity log, standard fields such as stage are updated from it, and fields can be refilled later by traversing the log. They "began with fully arbitrary schema" and avoid upfront schema design. — [a16z Show transcript](https://podscripts.co/podcasts/the-a16z-show/the-ai-native-crm)
- The homepage describes a "temporal context graph that tracks how data changes over time" alongside custom objects and relationships. — [Lightfield homepage](https://lightfield.com)
- Lightfield's own claim (biased): a "semi-structured business graph that stores raw interaction context. Schema-less, with custom fields that can be backfilled from past conversations." — [Lightfield blog: Day AI review](https://lightfield.com/blog/day-ai-review)
- Knowledge: Workspace and Personal knowledge files (company overview, ICP, pricing, competitors, templates) are used as agent context for meeting prep, drafts, chat and automations. — [Lightfield docs: Knowledge](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/knowledge/)

**Day AI: ingestion**
- Sources in Day AI's own comparison (Oct 2026): Gmail and Google Calendar, a native notetaker, Gong and Granola history, and Slack "from the moment the Day AI app joins a channel." — [Day AI vs Lightfield](https://day.ai/guides/day-ai-vs-lightfield)
- Microsoft 365 (Outlook mail, calendar and Teams) has been supported since Oct 6 2026, but "through MCP connectors" (the agent accesses the data via MCP), not native sync. Slack messages from apps and bots (Gong, Salesforce alerts, webhooks) now sync. — [Day AI update, Oct 6 2026](https://day.ai/resources/microsoft-users-can-now-use-day-ai)
- Meeting bot joins Zoom, Google Meet and Teams. Each user controls their own bot; there is no shared workspace bot. When several users record the same meeting they become co-owners of one recording. — [Day AI meeting bot guide, May 15 2026](https://day.ai/resources/meeting-recording-bot)
- Transcription in 20+ languages, with summaries in English (as of Nov 2024). — [Day AI update, Nov 20 2024](https://day.ai/resources/enrichment-and-3-more-quality-of-life-improvements)
- Seed-era description (Jun 2024): "Day AI creates an already-full CRM for you automatically, pulling from the natural conversations in your email and video meetings. Every relationship gets categorized and summarized." Day AI "updates your pipeline", and a screenshot shows a deal moved to Closed Won from a contract email. — [Day AI seed post, Jun 20 2024](https://day.ai/resources/day-ai-raises-4m-from-sequoia-capital-to-reimagine-crm-for-the-ai-age)
- Current guardrail example: agents create deals from reps' conversations and move them through stages, "and it stops before closed-won and closed-lost." — [Day AI security review guide, Nov 2 2026](https://day.ai/guides/ai-crm-security-review)
- Email privacy: each person sets sharing rules (share all or none by default, with exceptions by domain, address or Gmail label). "A private rule from any teammate keeps a message private, and one private message keeps its whole thread private." For meetings, the most private attendee setting wins. "New Day AI accounts start with email private and meetings invite only." Agents inherit the permissions of their person. — [Day AI security review guide](https://day.ai/guides/ai-crm-security-review)

**Day AI: data model and memory**
- "Customer Memory is an LLM-optimized ontology built from automatically permissioned ingestion of your team's email, calendar, meetings, and Slack" (site copy as quoted in search results). — [Day AI homepage via search](https://day.ai/)
- April 2026: Customer Memory is a "living record of every relationship": who said what, when, why, what they need, and what was promised. "Agents without memory are just prompts." — [Day AI blog, Apr 15 2026](https://www.day.ai/resources/announcing-customer-memory-for-agents)
- The context graph is nodes (people, companies, deals, conversations and events) and edges. Unlike a knowledge graph, it puts marks on every fact such as "When" and source, and "every value shows its sources, and you can ask the agent what changed and why." Each agent also keeps a memory of the person it works for. — [Day AI guide: What is a GTM context graph](https://day.ai/guides/what-is-a-context-graph)
- Public object types: `native_contact`, `native_organization`, `native_opportunity`, `native_pipeline`, `native_stage`, `native_meetingrecording`, `native_meetingrecordingclip`, `native_calendarevent`, `native_emailmessage`, `native_action` (tasks), `native_page`, `native_context` (notes), `native_draft`, `native_campaign`, `native_template`, `native_thread` (chat), `native_slackchannel`, `native_slackmessage` (60-day default lookback), `native_list`, `native_file`, `native_folder`, `native_view`, `native_user`, `native_assistant` (agents), `native_instruction`, `native_webpage` and `native_workspace`. Email and calendar participants are stored as email addresses and domains, not object IDs. — [Day AI SDK SCHEMA.md](https://github.com/day-ai/day-ai-sdk/blob/master/SCHEMA.md)
- Relationships are typed edges (e.g. opportunity `subject` → organization, contact `attended` → meeting recording) with their own property bag (`edgeProperties`, e.g. campaign-member status). History is exposed through `read_object_history` and `read_property_history`. — [Day AI SDK TOOLS.md](https://github.com/day-ai/day-ai-sdk/blob/master/TOOLS.md)
- Custom properties (picklist, text, number, date, currency and others) on opportunities, organizations and contacts. The property `description` "is actually instructions to an llm on how to fill out the property". `aiManaged` fills it in the background from emails, recordings, Slack, notes and calendar, `useWeb` searches the web, and `backfill_custom_property` runs a backfill job. — [Day AI SDK TOOLS.md](https://github.com/day-ai/day-ai-sdk/blob/master/TOOLS.md)
- "Pages" act as a permissioned knowledge graph for markdown "Second Brain" files. Pages link to contacts, organizations and opportunities, start private, and agents can read and edit them via MCP. — [Day AI SDK README](https://github.com/day-ai/day-ai-sdk)

### Inferences
- Lightfield's EAV-plus-typed-edges-plus-versioned-values design and Day AI's typed object graph with edge properties and per-property history solve the same problem: runtime-defined schema, a time dimension, and provenance for agents. Both keep the conversation corpus (emails, transcripts, Slack) as first-class linked objects instead of collapsing it into fields. "Keep everything the customer said" means storing the raw artifacts and linking them, then deriving fields with LLM fill and backfill.
- Lightfield's choice not to auto-create opportunities (and Day AI's toggle and closed-won guardrail) suggests both learned that auto-creating deals erodes pipeline trust. Bessemer reports this explicitly for Day AI.

### Gaps
- Neither vendor documents how it does entity resolution and identity merging (person and company matching across email aliases and domains) beyond domain-based account creation and merge tools.
- Day AI's physical storage for the graph is not documented. Only the subprocessor list hints at it (see section 7).

## 3. AI features: chat, agents, actions, meeting prep, forecasting, approvals, models

### Takeaway
Both offer chat over the whole CRM with citations, code execution (Python) for analysis, skills (saved instructions), scheduled and triggered automations, email drafting and sequences, meeting prep, and review queues. Lightfield centers on one workspace agent plus "automations" described in chat, with a For review queue and per-user Ask/Auto-run settings. Day AI centers on named per-person agents with job descriptions, skills on cron or event triggers, and tool-level Always allow / Needs approval / Off controls. Both use Anthropic and OpenAI models; neither trains its own foundation model.

### Cited Findings
**Lightfield**
- The agent answers questions over accounts, contacts, opportunities, emails, meetings, transcripts and notes. It updates records, drafts emails, creates tasks and builds reports, researches the web on every plan (multi-step research tasks), and checks email deliverability for up to 50 addresses. Long jobs (imports, CSV exports, batch enrichment, multi-company research) run in the background. — [Lightfield docs: What the agent can do](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/chat/)
- The homepage lists an agent harness with a standardized SDK, a deterministic code sandbox and continuous evals. Agents run outbound, flag at-risk deals and suggest next steps. Skills include account research, deal qualification, meeting prep, follow-up drafts and a "Deal review" skill against a qualification framework. "Ask Lightfield" chat covers pipeline, churn and account history, and reporting covers pipeline coverage, forecast tracking and ARR. — [Lightfield homepage](https://lightfield.com)
- The agent "runs Python code against full customer history" and produces artifacts such as battle cards and pipeline reports (Lightfield's own claim). — [Lightfield blog: Day AI review](https://lightfield.com/blog/day-ai-review)
- Skills are saved instructions in three tiers: Workspace (admin-edited), Personal and System (read-only). They are created by chatting with a built-in "Create skill" skill. — [Lightfield docs: Skills](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/skills/)
- Automations replace the older visual Workflows: "you describe the process in chat and the agent builds and tests it with you." Workflow triggers include record created/updated, meeting scheduled, task/note events, webhook received, and schedule (cron). Any step can be an AI agent step. — [Lightfield docs: Workflows](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/settings/workflows/)
- Approvals: For review holds email and LinkedIn drafts from automations and sequences. Approving an automation email sends it; approving a sequence draft queues it under the sequence's pacing. Drafts go to specific reviewers (the sending-mailbox owner, the sequence creator, extra reviewers). Only the mailbox owner can send. To skip review, the user asks the agent to change the automation and approves its "email-sending permission". — [Lightfield docs: For review](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/for-review/); [Who reviews a draft](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/for-review/who-reviews-a-draft/)
- In chat, Record creation and updates defaults to Ask every time (confirmation cards), with Auto-run optional. Deletes always need confirmation. Outbound HTTP requests from chat default to Ask every time; Auto-approve is gated behind support. "Bulk or scripted changes through the API can apply without individual confirmation cards." — [Lightfield docs: Agent settings](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/settings/agent/)
- Suggested record updates and tasks wait on the record and on "Up next", not in For review. — [Lightfield docs: For review](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/for-review/)
- Target accounts: a 0–100 score with rationale, combining ICP fit, timing signals (e.g. "Fresh Raise", funding in the last 90 days) and strategic factors. The agent fills account status (New … Churned). — [Lightfield docs: Target accounts](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/target-accounts/)
- Models: chat defaults to "Auto" (Lightfield picks the model and may change it). Record lookups during a task run on Auto. Premium models, available on Pro and above, are listed as Claude Opus 4.8, Claude Opus 5, Claude Opus 5.5, Claude Fable 5, GPT-5.5, GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-6 Astra. — [Lightfield docs: Choose a model](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/chat/choose-a-model/); [Premium models](https://docs.lightfield.app/help/chat/premium-models/)
- "Lightfield does not train its own LLMs yet": Peiris says "$47 million is not enough for that." — [Upstarts Media](https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/ai-sales-startup-lightfield-raises-47m)
- A research lab ("Lightfield Research") reports to co-founder and Chief AI Officer Ves Stoyanov (ex-Facebook AI, co-author of RoBERTa and XLM-R). It works on LLM-as-judge and rubric evals, benchmarks for list building, CSV imports, mail intelligence, record updates and outbound, simulated companies with known answers, and RL and fine-tuning "where it pays off". It also models deal probability and ships it as product features. — [Lightfield Research Scientist job](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Lightfield/4b300dfd-b185-46a6-9056-13ae1a71dc75)
- Applied AI engineers "build and evolve our agent harness: the agent loop, tools, skills, and memory" and improve quality, cost and latency through evals, simulation environments and experiments. — [Lightfield Applied AI job](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Lightfield/b32dc57b-ffa0-4bb7-99c8-f469c3876002)
- Workflows are created in chat as a "recipe" the agent writes from the world model. — [a16z Show transcript](https://podscripts.co/podcasts/the-a16z-show/the-ai-native-crm)
- Simulation and predictive features were "not yet in use" by the customers Upstarts interviewed (Sep 2026). — [Upstarts Media](https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/ai-sales-startup-lightfield-raises-47m)

**Day AI**
- Agents are named assistants with a first and last name, title, job description, DISC personality profile and language, managed with `assistant_settings`. Each reports to one person and "works with the permissions of the person it works for". — [Day AI SDK TOOLS.md](https://github.com/day-ai/day-ai-sdk/blob/master/TOOLS.md); [Day AI security review guide](https://day.ai/guides/ai-crm-security-review)
- Skills: triggers are SCHEDULE (15-minute-aligned cron; daily, weekdays, weekly or every 4 hours), EVENT (e.g. `SLACK_SUMMARY_READY`) or NEITHER. Notifications go by email or Slack. A skill can include up to 5 other skills, attach Pages and Folders, and limit itself to specific MCP connectors (`mcpProviders`). A workspace Skill Library deploys skills to teammates' agents as Managed (stays synced) or Template (forks). Prompts are versioned and restorable. Campaign drafting skills are auto-provisioned. Automated skill slots are capped per tier. — [Day AI SDK TOOLS.md](https://github.com/day-ai/day-ai-sdk/blob/master/TOOLS.md)
- Delegation: background tasks can pause with questions, and `respond_to_delegation` resumes them. — [Day AI SDK TOOLS.md](https://github.com/day-ai/day-ai-sdk/blob/master/TOOLS.md)
- Governance: every tool on a connected MCP server is set to Always allow / Needs approval / Off. By default reads are allowed and writes need approval, shown as Approve/Deny cards in chat. Skills can start in "learning mode", reporting what they would have done before they may act. Skills send approval requests by email or Slack, and every skill keeps a run history. — [Day AI guide: control AI agents' CRM access](https://day.ai/guides/control-ai-agents-crm-access); [Day AI security review guide](https://day.ai/guides/ai-crm-security-review)
- Citations: answers cite the call clip or message they came from. — [Day AI security review guide](https://day.ai/guides/ai-crm-security-review); [Customer Memory post](https://www.day.ai/resources/announcing-customer-memory-for-agents)
- Default agent skills and uses: a Morning Briefing skill ships with every new agent (Apr 2026). Agents draft follow-up emails, brief before meetings, review quiet deals and draft outreach, and flag at-risk customers with outreach drafted "for user approval". — [Day AI update, Apr 23 2026](https://day.ai/resources/mcp-client-code-execution); [Customer Memory post](https://www.day.ai/resources/announcing-customer-memory-for-agents)
- Code execution: "It writes Python, runs it against your actual data, and builds the chart from real numbers." `export_to_sandbox` exports CRM data as CSV for a later `execute_code` turn, and the sandbox vendor is E2B (see section 7). — [Day AI update, Apr 23 2026](https://day.ai/resources/mcp-client-code-execution); [TOOLS.md](https://github.com/day-ai/day-ai-sdk/blob/master/TOOLS.md)
- Email drafting guardrails: by default a draft is refused when a To address has never emailed the workspace but another address on the same contact has. Generic addresses (info@ etc.), duplicate drafts and missing greetings need explicit overrides. Campaign drafts carry a "Why now" candidate and a draft explanation. — [Day AI SDK TOOLS.md](https://github.com/day-ai/day-ai-sdk/blob/master/TOOLS.md)
- Campaigns and outbound sequences: draft, refine and regenerate campaign outreach, with bulk regeneration of unapproved drafts after "explicit user confirmation". — [Day AI SDK TOOLS.md](https://github.com/day-ai/day-ai-sdk/blob/master/TOOLS.md)
- Reporting: weighted forecasts and time-in-stage reports ("Introducing Reports & Analytics", undated in the index), plus charts and widgets via `create_chart` and `create_widget`. — [Day AI llms.txt](https://day.ai/llms.txt); [TOOLS.md](https://github.com/day-ai/day-ai-sdk/blob/master/TOOLS.md)
- Models: inference subprocessors are Anthropic, OpenAI, Baseten, Fireworks AI and TypeSafe AI, with AI monitoring by Braintrust. — [Day AI subprocessors](https://day.ai/trust/subprocessors). The SDK recommends `claude-sonnet-5-5` as "the default for Day AI agents", `claude-opus-5-5` for long research, and `claude-haiku-5-5` for high-volume jobs. ## 9. Day AI "CRMx" (and successor concepts) and Lightfield's pivot from Tome

### Takeaway
"CRMx" ("x" for context) was Day AI's Feb 2026 GA label for a CRM built for agents on a context graph. By late 2026 it had been folded into "Customer Memory" (Apr 2026), "context graph and agent control plane", "company brain" and the "Life After CRM" manifesto. Lightfield is Tome's team and investors re-founded on the lesson that LLMs lack the context to finish work. The pivot path ran through GTM pilots and a "go-to-market assistant" with no pricing power before the CRM rebuild.

### Cited Findings
**Day AI: CRMx and related names**
- The "x" in CRMx "stands for 'context'". The Series A post closes with "CRMx is here". Day AI was described as "the first CRMx platform". — [Day AI Series A post](https://www.day.ai/resources/series-a-and-the-beginning-of-the-shift-in-crm); [Upstarts Media](https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/day-ai-sequoia-ai-crm)
- June 2024 category name: "a new type of CRM that we call Customer Intelligence", combining a CRM, a meeting assistant and a knowledge base. — [Day AI seed post](https://day.ai/resources/day-ai-raises-4m-from-sequoia-capital-to-reimagine-crm-for-the-ai-age)
- April 2026: "Customer Memory for agents". — [Day AI blog](https://www.day.ai/resources/announcing-customer-memory-for-agents)
- Late 2026 language: "context graph and agent control plane", "company brain", "Life After CRM" manifesto, "Operations Harness" (site footer), "Second Brain" on Pages, an Agent Grader site, and an open-source "company-brain-evaluation" skill (`npx skills add day-ai/company-brain-evaluation`). — [Day AI llms.txt](https://day.ai/llms.txt); [Day AI About](https://day.ai/about); [Day AI SDK](https://github.com/day-ai/day-ai-sdk)

**Lightfield: pivot from Tome**
- Mar 2025, Peiris: "We started to build Tome in 2020. 5 years and 25M users later, it's become clear to us that LLMs are good at starting work, but lack the context required to finish it." — [Keith Peiris on X](https://x.com/keithpeiris/status/1902817105264906655)
- Tome history: founded 2020 by Meta alumni Peiris and Liriani, incubated at Greylock with Reid Hoffman on the board. It reached about 25M users, made the Forbes AI 50 in 2023 and 2024, and raised about $81M at a $300M valuation. — [Open Source CEO](https://www.opensourceceo.com/p/keith-peiris-interview). Peak of about 2M monthly users per the podcast — [a16z Show transcript](https://podscripts.co/podcasts/the-a16z-show/the-ai-native-crm)
- Pivot path: sales and marketing users, then 12 pilots at 500–1,000-person companies, then a "go-to-market assistant" that "lacked pricing power", then a CRM rebuilt from first principles. — [a16z Show transcript](https://podscripts.co/podcasts/the-a16z-show/the-ai-native-crm)
- What carried over: the co-founders, the team (cut from 70 to 6 or 7), investor relationships (Coatue's Dan Rose backed the pivot; Greylock, Lightspeed and Coatue reinvested in the Series A), and the "follow the heat" approach. First customers were offered free office space in exchange for using the CRM and providing training data. — [Open Source CEO](https://www.opensourceceo.com/p/keith-peiris-interview); [Upstarts Media](https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/ai-sales-startup-lightfield-raises-47m); [a16z Show transcript](https://podscripts.co/podcasts/the-a16z-show/the-ai-native-crm)
- Peiris: "It was two years of pain, but we're now closer-aligned to the company that fits our life's work." Gamma took Tome's presentation market. — [Upstarts Media](https://www.upstartsmedia.com/p/ai-sales-startup-lightfield-raises-47m)

### Inferences
- No Tome technology appears to have carried over into Lightfield's CRM stack: the sources mention only team, investors and lessons. The idea that "context is the bottleneck for LLM work" is the throughline into Lightfield's world-model data layer.
- Day AI's naming churn (Customer Intelligence → AI-native CRM → CRMx → Customer Memory → context graph/agent control plane) tracks its move from replacing CRM to also sitting on top of incumbents.

### Gaps
- No exact Tome shutdown date was found. The X post dates the announcement to March 2025; whether the slides product closed later was not confirmed.
